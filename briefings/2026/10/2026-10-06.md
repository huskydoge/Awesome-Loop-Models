# Daily Loop-Model Briefing - 2026-10-06

## Outcome

Six papers were added. Each reuses a learned block, update rule, or coupled module
several times within one forward pass. Three concern looped language models (compression,
a decision readout, and the KV cache); the others loop a refinement block for audio
language models, iterate a deformable cellular-automaton rule on puzzles, and fuse two
modalities at a fixed point.

## Looped language models

### Why compressed loops collapse

[A Tilted Bowl Is Not a Slippery Slope](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39277) compresses more
than 30 looped models from five families (HRM and TRM puzzle reasoners, recurrent convnets,
deep equilibrium models, looped language models such as Huginn and Ouro, and diffusion
samplers as a boundary case). Rounding error accumulates only in loops that never settle.
When a loop settles, a fixed weight error moves the point where it settles, and the answer
is lost only when that shift exceeds what the readout tolerates. A controller that stops
when the halting head fires and then runs a few loops with 8-bit weights lifts 20
compressed Sudoku-Extreme models from 61.1 to 75.8 percent at under a third of the weight
traffic of fixed-depth inference.

### A typed decision readout after every loop

[SanSi](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07730) keeps Ouro-1.4B frozen, adds LoRA adapters and a
small readout (61M trainable parameters), and reads option probabilities after each pass
of the 24-layer stack, training every loop with a proper scoring rule so one model serves
budgets from one to eight loops. On 10,027 test decisions from 59 sources it reaches 72.0
percent, 13.5 points above a non-looped model of the same shape trained with the same
recipe and 1.8 points below a single-pass model with three times the parameters
([project page](https://minnesotanlp.github.io/Sansi/)).

### Shrinking the per-loop KV cache

[Hybrid Latent Attention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07940) keeps exact keys and values for
a sliding window of recent tokens and stores each older token as a latent that the query
of every loop reads directly, without reconstructing keys and values. Uptrained on frozen
four-loop Ouro-1.4B and Ouro-2.6B, it shrinks the cache 10.7x per token, fits 4.0-8.8x as
many concurrent sequences per GPU, speeds decoding 2.5x at 1K and up to 7.4x at 16K
context, and keeps over 97 percent of the original accuracy on math, knowledge, and
reasoning benchmarks.

## Architectures and applications

### Adaptive-depth loops for audio language models

[AdaLoop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06949) inserts a recurrent latent block between the
audio encoder and the language model. The block's cross-attention, multi-scale local
self-attention, and feed-forward update share weights across up to eight iterations, and a
question-conditioned halting unit decides when to stop. Adding under 3 percent parameters,
it raises average accuracy on MMSU, MMAU-Pro, and MMAR by 2.9-3.8 points across
Qwen2.5-Omni, Kimi-Audio, and MiMo-Audio, with harder perception questions receiving more
iterations.

### Deformable neural cellular automata for puzzles

[Adaptive Neural Cellular Automata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08518) give the perception
step of a neural cellular automaton deformable convolutions whose offsets come from the
current state, so the same update rule, iterated over many steps, can reach beyond its
local neighborhood. With about 165k parameters it solves 87.8 percent of hard array Sudokus
(78.2 for a 3x3-convolution NCA), and with 256k parameters it finds valid optimal maze
paths 94.2 percent of the time ([code](https://github.com/mspitzna/NCAtorch)).

### Multimodal fusion at a fixed point

[Mutual Equilibrium](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39456) couples two modality-specific modules
whose outputs feed back into each other and takes the fixed point of this interaction as
the joint embedding, a special case of a deep equilibrium model computed by damped
fixed-point iteration with a residual penalty. It outperforms concatenation fusion on
multimodal classification benchmarks, with the largest gain on VQA v2, and progressively
refines visual grounding over iterations.

## Near misses

- [Recurrent Looped Transformer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07591) splits eight layers into
  a parallel causal encoder and a decoder that merges each token with the previous token's
  final decoder state, generalizing parity to 256 bits. The recurrence runs across tokens,
  not by reapplying layers within a token's forward pass.
- [Continuous Memory Machines](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07907) extend the Continuous
  Thought Machine with matrix-valued short- and long-term memory updated by a Transformer;
  the recurrence is over input timesteps rather than a reused block on one input.
- [D-Loop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06011) and [DLoop](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07659)
  improve speculative decoding by running a drafter for extra passes before verification;
  each loop is another call of the drafter, not a reused internal block.

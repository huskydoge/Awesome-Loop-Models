# Daily Loop-Model Briefing - 2026-10-08

## Outcome

Five papers were added. Each reapplies a shared learned block within one forward pass.
Two make looped language models cheaper to serve, one retrofits per-token recursion into
a multimodal LLM, and two apply looped reasoners to image generation and tabular
prediction.

## Serving looped language models

### Early exit that actually skips work

[Enabling Dynamic Computation in Looped LMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09013) notes
that Ouro's released code still runs all four loops for every token, because a later
token that reaches loop d needs loop-d keys and values from every earlier token. Ragged
KV lets that token read the earlier token's exit-depth entries instead, so exited tokens
never compute deeper loops. Out of the box it cuts FLOPs and KV memory by up to 30
percent while keeping full-depth accuracy, and ragged-aware training narrows the
remaining gap. The paper also shows that Ouro's per-token entropy prior pushes all
tokens toward the same exit depth; applying the KL to the batch-average exit
distribution instead, tested on 160M looped models pretrained from scratch on 20B
FineWeb-edu tokens, gives confident and difficulty-dependent exits.

### Quantizing the loop-wise KV cache

[ResidualQuant](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10381) observes that a looped transformer's
keys and values change little from loop to loop. It keeps the final loop's KV as an
anchor and stores the other loops as low-bit residuals, adding least-squares scaling,
rotations, and loop-wise mixed precision. On Ouro-1.4B (4 loops) and Huginn-3.5B (32
loops) over GSM8K, MATH500, HumanEval, and MBPP it stays close to BF16 while cutting
theoretical KV storage by 80.7 percent, scores up to 13.0 points above a rotation-based
baseline at equal memory, and raises fixed-batch decode throughput on an RTX 5090 by up
to 2.73x.

## Retrofitting recursion into a multimodal LLM

[MoR-MLLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08830) keeps the first and last layers of a Phi-2
backbone, replaces the 30 middle layers with five shared recursive blocks, and lets a
router decide per visual or text token whether to take another recursion step. A
three-stage recipe (projector warm-up, router warm-up, joint tuning with LoRA and an
entropy routing loss) stabilizes training. Against LLaVA-1.5-7B it reduces inference
TFLOPs by 86.6 percent and GPU memory by 54.1 percent on average across five benchmarks
while staying competitive on nine. At matched compute it beats a fixed-depth recursive
baseline.

## Applications

### A recursive reasoner inside each denoising step

[Painter-Thinker](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09876) attaches a TRM-style recursive
network (the Thinker) to a frozen diffusion UNet (the Painter). Within each denoising
step the Thinker refines a latent state over learned tokens that encode the noisy image
and the condition, then steers the Painter through ControlNet adapters. Training uses
only the reconstruction loss. With 10M parameters it solves 92.5 percent of hard and
71.2 percent of extreme MNIST Sudoku puzzles, against 75 and 4.1 percent for the prior
best. It also improves on mazes, Queens, and CLEVR spatial-relation scenes, and it
recovers from injected mistakes that the plain diffusion model cannot fix.

### Looping a tabular in-context learner

[TAFFY](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07559) runs one shared twelve-block in-context
transformer for several passes over the table's support and query rows. A gate computed
from support-set statistics scales the final pass's update. Its synthetic pretraining
contexts mix several related causal environments. With four loops it gets the lowest
average rank on six classification suites, and the regression model ranks first on five
suites. Training with more loops helps, but adding loops only at inference does not.

## Near misses

- [LayerRoPE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09179) replaces per-layer normalization gains
  with one shared vector plus depth-conditioned scalars and reports gains when transferred
  to looped models. Its subject is normalization in untied stacks, not a reused block.
- [Input-Blind Controls for Layer Programs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10368) shows that
  input-blind perturbations recover as much oracle headroom as selected layer-skip and
  layer-repeat programs on Qwen3-4B-Base and Llama-3.1-8B. It is an evaluation critique,
  not a loop-model method or analysis.
- [Thinking in Depth (Retro)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10317) lets later layers of a
  tabular foundation model reweight earlier representations through attention residuals.
  The layers are not reused.
- [FLaRe](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06666) studies flow matching in a learned latent
  space for LLM latent reasoning. Its iterations are flow-sampling steps, not a reused
  internal block.

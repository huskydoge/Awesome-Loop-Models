# Daily Loop-Model Briefing - 2026-10-29

## Outcome

Four papers were added, each applying a shared block repeatedly inside one forward pass in a
different domain: speech recognition on edge devices, robot manipulation, a looped hybrid
language model, and boundary refinement for remote-sensing segmentation. The robot policy and
the segmentation refiner were posted earlier in the month and are included now. None of the four cites Benhao Huang's
work.

## Added

### Depth through Recurrence: Towards Ultra-Efficient On-Device ASR
[arXiv:2610.37833](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37833) · Code: none stated in the paper

**TL;DR:** Deep speech encoders are distilled into a handful of shared Conformer blocks, each
applied several times in a row, keeping the teacher's depth and most of its accuracy at a
fraction of the parameters.

- **Setup:** Students distilled from STT-480ms (streaming, 108.9M encoder parameters) and
  Parakeet-TDT-0.6B-v2 (608.9M), trained on a seven-corpus public mixture and scored by word
  error rate on the eight Open ASR Leaderboard benchmarks.
- **Mechanism / Method:** Layer-wise CKA similarity splits the teacher's encoder into K
  contiguous groups; each group becomes one shared block, initialized as the average of its
  teacher layers and applied as many times as the group had layers. Training combines the ASR
  loss with encoder distillation at group boundaries and decoder KL distillation. A
  speaker-conditioned MoE-LoRA extension personalizes the frozen backbone.
- **Results:** With K = 2 (14.2M) the STT student reaches 13.40% average WER against the
  teacher's 11.05%, which the paper reports as 97.4% of its accuracy; K = 4 (26.8M) reaches
  10.72%. A four-block Parakeet student (105M) reaches 7.62% against 6.05%. Weight reuse cuts
  encoder latency by up to 1.21x on GPU and 1.03-1.07x on CPU at comparable FLOPs.
- **Caveat:** Several training corpora overlap the teachers' own training data and the
  benchmarks' domains, so students that beat the teacher do not show recurrence beating untied
  depth; there is no untied student of the same size.
- **Why added:** Every student encoder is a short sequence of weight-shared blocks, each
  reused several times within one forward pass.

### Tiny Recursive Policy: Recursive Latent Refinement for Visuomotor Control
[arXiv:2610.33288](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33288) · Code: none stated in the paper

**TL;DR:** A behaviour-cloning policy refines a pair of latent states with one small shared
block in nested cycles at every control step and carries them forward, giving strong results
with a few million parameters.

- **Setup:** Multi-task and five-shot behaviour cloning on LIBERO-90, LIBERO-LONG, MetaWorld
  ML45 and the memory-dependent LIBERO-Mem, against ResNet-T, ACT, VQ-BeT, QueST, Diffusion
  Policy, MIP and a flow policy.
- **Mechanism / Method:** In the style of Tiny Recursive Models, one shared module updates a
  low-level latent K times inside each of R high-level cycles (default three cycles of six
  steps), with gradients only through the last cycle. The final high-level state is decoded
  into an action chunk and both states carry over to the next control step.
- **Results:** The 4.2M-parameter backbone scores 0.929 on LIBERO-90 and 0.919 on LIBERO-LONG,
  ahead of the 336.3M flow policy (0.899 and 0.897), and 0.597 on LIBERO-Mem against about
  0.41 for the best baseline. With a single cycle the carry hurts: LIBERO-LONG falls to 0.206.
- **Caveat:** All results are in simulation, and extra cycles beyond the default reduce
  performance rather than helping.
- **Why added:** The same learned block is applied repeatedly within each policy evaluation.

### Think Twice: The Metis-1.6 Technical Report
[arXiv:2610.37903](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37903) · Models: [huggingface.co/Lernex](https://huggingface.co/Lernex)

**TL;DR:** Two language models trained from scratch by a single author run their whole hybrid
Mamba-2/attention mixture-of-experts stack twice per token, and the report details what did
and did not work.

- **Setup:** Praxis (2.94B parameters, 12 layers) and Logos (10.23B, 20 layers), each trained
  on 1.02T tokens including context extension to 131,072 tokens and supervised fine-tuning,
  evaluated on 40 post-training benchmarks, plus 10B-token proxy ablations of the loop design.
- **Mechanism / Method:** Every token makes exactly two passes through the same stack. All
  weights are shared except a pass-indexed rank-8 low-rank update on attention and pass
  embeddings, and a gated cross-pass memory lets the second pass read what the first wrote.
- **Results:** Logos averages 52.93 across the benchmarks, seventh of 17 comparison models,
  and scores 67.50% on AIME 2025. In the ablations the tied two-pass loop (2.5993 nats) trails
  an unrolled one-pass model with twice the parameters (2.5774), while a successor design with
  per-pass routers, a cross-pass highway and untied mixers reaches 2.5529.
- **Caveat:** The comparison against open models is not controlled for data or recipe, the
  report is weak on code, instruction following and long context, and the unrolled-versus-
  looped results come only from small proxy runs.
- **Why added:** Both released models apply one weight-shared stack twice in every forward
  pass, and the report studies how tying, untying and memory across passes change the result.

### Multiclass Boundary Refinement with Neural Cellular Automata for Remote Sensing Land Cover Segmentation
[arXiv:2610.30007](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30007) · Code: none stated in the paper

**TL;DR:** A neural cellular automaton repeatedly applies one small learned local rule to a
frozen segmenter's class probabilities to move land-cover boundaries closer to the truth.

- **Setup:** A frozen DPT-DINOv2 segmenter on LandCover.ai, scored by Boundary IoU, with an
  ablation over the number of hidden channels and the rule's width.
- **Mechanism / Method:** A shallow MLP shared by every cell updates the probabilities and
  free hidden channels from fixed neighbourhood filters and an image context vector, keeping
  the probabilities on the simplex with a log-space update. The rule runs 32 to 64 steps in
  training, trained by backpropagation through time, and 64 steps at inference.
- **Results:** The best setting lifts mean Boundary IoU from 46.63 to 48.99, with +8.5 points
  on woodland and +0.54 points of overall mIoU.
- **Caveat:** One segmenter, one dataset and no comparison with other boundary refiners; the
  gain peaks at the training step count and decays if the automaton runs longer.
- **Why added:** One learned update rule is iterated many times inside the refiner's forward
  pass.

## Near misses

- **Loopholing All the Way** ([2610.30068](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30068)): a diffusion
  language model that carries expected embeddings between denoising steps; each step is a
  full denoiser call, which is diffusion-style iterative inference.
- **ParaTTT** ([2610.38255](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38255)): parallelizes test-time
  training layers by fixed-point refinement of the inner-loop gradients; the recurrence is a
  sequence memory across tokens, not a reused block over depth.
- **Vision-Language Agents Can Learn in Their Own Latent Worlds**
  ([2610.36925](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36925)): uses a VLM as a latent transition model
  for model-based reinforcement learning, which calls the full model repeatedly.
- **Latent Visual Contrastive Reasoning with Multi-Branch Exploration**
  ([2610.36809](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36809)): latent visual reasoning branches trained
  by fine-tuning, with no internal block reused.

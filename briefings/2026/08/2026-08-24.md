# Daily Loop-Model Briefing - 2026-08-24

## Outcome

Two application papers were added. Both unroll a compact iterative computation
inside one forward process and explicitly reuse the same learned module at each
refinement step.

## Added

### CAIR (`2608.21207`)

CAIR imputes missing physiological measurements in two stages. A bidirectional
GRU first predicts a coarse interpolation, then one tied-weight Transformer
refiner corrects that estimate over three passes, each conditioned on the
previous prediction.

Why it was added: the refiner is a single learned operator reused across
recurrent depth, rather than three independently parameterized stages.

Reader takeaway: on AI-READI continuous glucose monitoring, CAIR reports the
lowest reconstruction error under MCAR, MAR, and NMAR missingness. Its margin
over linear interpolation grows from 9% to 16% to 19% as missingness becomes
more difficult. On MIMIC-III, the advantage transfers to arterial pressure,
while heart rate remains a boundary case where interpolation can still be
competitive.

### Frozen CLIP Priors for Robust Self-Supervised Poisson Inverse Problems (`2608.20524`)

This work combines closed-form ADMM data-consistency updates with a frozen CLIP
RN50 encoder and a lightweight learned decoder. The decoder weights are shared
across every unrolled reconstruction iteration: two passes for demosaicing and
three for deblurring.

Why it was added: the learned CLIP-based prior is repeatedly applied within the
finite-depth solver, satisfying the catalog's learned-module reuse rule.

Reader takeaway: the self-supervised variant remains close to supervised
training across Poisson demosaicing and deblurring. Under severe noise it
improves BSDS500 demosaicing PSNR by 0.48 dB over the strongest PSNR baseline,
while the reported 0.0242-second runtime is substantially lower than the
iterative plug-and-play baselines evaluated by the authors.

## Near misses

- `2608.20942` unfolds a PDE-inspired solver, but the paper does not establish
  that its learned operators are tied across stages.
- `2608.21308` and `2608.20961` use recurrence over sequence time rather than
  recurrent computation depth.
- `2608.21315` studies fixed points produced by repeated full-model decoding;
  it does not introduce an implicit or reused internal layer.
- `2608.21265` retrieves external reasoning memories as prompt-side scaffolds,
  without internal learned-module recurrence.
- `2608.21345` is a generation-critique-revision pipeline, and `2608.20839`
  relies on diffusion-style iterative inference.

# Daily Loop-Model Briefing - 2026-08-02

## Outcome

Three recent papers were added today. **Looped Transformers with Source-Centered State Evolution** is a direct looped-Transformer architecture paper. **ReLoop-UME** applies recurrent-depth computation to universal multimodal embedding. **SILVA Networks** is an implicit-layer entry centered on trainable fixed-point interaction dynamics.

## Added

### Looped Transformers with Source-Centered State Evolution (`2607.27656`)

This paper starts from a standard looped-Transformer problem: reusing the same Transformer block over recurrent depth gives a train- and test-time compute axis, but the shared transition must behave sensibly across a whole trajectory of hidden states. Source-Centered State Evolution keeps an input-conditioned learned anchor, represents recurrent motion as deviations from that anchor, and forces the zero-deviation state to stay fixed.

Why it was added: the abstract explicitly centers on reusing a learned Transformer block across recurrent depth within one model forward process. SCSE is a design change to that internal loop, not an external prompting or agent workflow.

Reader takeaway: the paper reports improvements to the controlled recurrent quality frontier across WikiText-2, WikiText-103, web-corpus pretraining, held-out web-text transfer, and LAMBADA completion, with ablations pointing to the learned anchor and anchor-coordinate recurrence as key contributors.

### ReLoop-UME: Recurrent Depth with Learnable Retrieval Registers for Universal Multimodal Embedding (`2607.28751`)

ReLoop-UME asks whether universal multimodal embedding can spend extra computation along model depth while keeping the token workspace fixed. It executes early layers once, recurrently reuses a parameter-shared retrieval-forming block, and uses learnable retrieval registers as persistent states that accumulate evidence across loops before the final embedding readout.

Why it was added: the mechanism is explicit recurrent-depth reuse of a learned internal block, with learned register states evolving across loop steps. That puts it inside the repository's strict scope even though the task is retrieval rather than language modeling.

Reader takeaway: the abstract reports consistent retrieval gains on MMEB-V2 and MRMR, while running much faster than latent-autoregressive or rationale-token UME alternatives.

### SILVA Networks as Structured Implicit Layers and Vector Attractors via Dynamic Interaction Fields (`2607.28989`)

SILVA Networks introduce a fixed-point architecture that separates stimulus, local interaction, global interaction, damping, and readout terms. The same implicit-layer template is instantiated across images, molecules, citation networks, and long-range graph benchmarks, with ablations used to diagnose which terms carry the task.

Why it was added: fixed-point implicit-layer dynamics are the central architecture. The paper is not merely using an iterative solver around a non-looped model; it presents the trainable representation itself as structured implicit dynamics.

Reader takeaway: the strongest result is interpretability and diagnosis of implicit-layer dynamics rather than a single broad benchmark win: local interactions are load-bearing in graph tasks, MNIST gains little from recurrence at the tested capacity, and global interaction helps most clearly on a long-range node-classification benchmark.

## Checked but not added

- **The Grokked Illusion** (`2607.29503`) studies high-entropy model robustness and catastrophic forgetting after grokking. It is useful robustness work, but the abstract does not establish reused internal learned-operator recurrence.
- **Learning Latent Reasoning Traces for Scalar Reward Models End-to-End** (`2607.29185`) learns discrete latent reasoning traces for reward modeling. It remains outside the main list unless the paper text shows a reused learned recurrent block/operator rather than latent-variable optimization.
- **EvoReason** (`2607.29010`), **OPLD** (`2607.28154`), and **Hierarchical Latent Reasoning for LLM-based Recommendation** (`2607.27760`) are adjacent latent-reasoning papers, but the abstracts do not clearly cross the repo's same-forward reused-module boundary.

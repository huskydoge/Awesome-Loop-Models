# Daily Loop-Model Briefing - 2026-08-26

## Outcome

Two papers were added. One steers explicit recurrent-depth trajectories at inference
time; the other stabilizes backward responses through an implicit fixed-point layer.
Both act on learned computation that is reused within one model forward process.

## Added

### Steering Recurrent Reasoners at Inference Time with Readout Feedback (`2608.24136`)

Readout Feedback converts a recurrent reasoner's intermediate output probabilities into
token-wise pairwise coupling forces and injects them back into its latent dynamics. It
requires no retraining and complements the usual strategy of simply running more
recurrent steps or sampling more trajectories.

Why it was added: the evaluated models repeatedly update their latent states with shared
learned computation blocks, and the proposed method directly controls that recurrent
depth from inside the trajectory.

Reader takeaway: across AKOrN, ItrSA++, and TRM on Sudoku and Maze, Readout Feedback
improves four of six model-task pairs. The authors report solutions that extra recurrent
steps or multi-trajectory selection do not reach at comparable or lower compute.

### Response Renormalization for Critical Deep Equilibrium Models (`2608.23725`)

Deep equilibrium models predict from a hidden state fixed by a learned update operator.
When the residual Jacobian is nearly singular along task-sensitive directions, implicit
differentiation can amplify small perturbations into unstable gradients. Collective Mode
Response Renormalization selectively lifts those near-pole response channels while
leaving well-conditioned channels unchanged.

Why it was added: the learned fixed-point operator is the model's central implicit layer,
and the paper specifically addresses how to train through its equilibrium reliably.

Reader takeaway: over 23 multiphysics families, the response-renormalized variants stay
within five percent of exact-implicit-differentiation test error in more than 98% of
static and 95% of transient family-seed comparisons. The result is broad controlled
evidence, although it remains concentrated in the author's DEQ/SILVA model family.

## Near misses

- `2608.24319` studies a certifiable homeostatic field that governs an adaptive-depth
  recurrent reasoner. It is highly relevant, but the field is presented as a compute
  governor rather than the reasoning operator itself, so its catalog placement needs a
  separate mechanism-level decision.
- `2608.23956` defines recursive operators over agent reasoning traces and evaluates them
  through repeated model calls, which is outside the internal-module reuse scope.
- `2608.22090` repeatedly denoises natural-language reasoning trajectories through
  predicted semantic operators; it belongs to iterative denoising rather than recurrent
  learned depth.

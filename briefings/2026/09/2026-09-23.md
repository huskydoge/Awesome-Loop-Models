# Daily Loop-Model Briefing - 2026-09-23

## Outcome

Today's additions form an unusually broad loop-model batch. Two papers sharpen the
theory of recurrent computation, four study recurrent-depth language or reasoning
models, and four apply shared internal iteration to forecasting, cellular systems,
control, and 3D reconstruction.

## Added

### Completion and statistical efficiency

[When Recursive Models Finish Computing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26487) treats
additional recurrence as a measurable computation trajectory rather than assuming a
fixed step budget is sufficient. Extending Tiny Recursive Models from 16 to 512 steps
raises cumulative exact Sudoku solve rates from 59.2% to 87.5% and from 74.4% to 91.9%
for the two reported models. Its stability analysis suggests that useful convergence
is direction-dependent rather than ordinary isotropic contraction.

[Statistical Gains from Looped Estimation under Parameter Budgets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25778)
provides complementary theory: under fixed parameter budgets, repeated reuse of the
same estimator map can achieve a strictly better risk regime than non-looped
alternatives and approach minimax rates with bounded parameters. Together, these papers
clarify both when to continue a loop and why sharing the loop can help.

### Recurrent-depth language and reasoning models

[LoopCD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24196) contrasts the logits produced at successive
recurrent depths, using earlier-loop predictions as an amateur distribution. It reports
1.06 to 6.71 percentage-point reasoning gains with about 0.98 to 0.99 times baseline
throughput. [WaveFront Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23033) attacks latency by
batching tokens at different sequence positions and recurrence depths, reporting 2.42x
speedup for Ouro and 3.54x for Huginn, or 4.81x with KV sharing.

[Dissecting Hierarchical Reasoning Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22197) uses
interventions, probes, and sparse autoencoders to identify constraint-aware iterative
refinement in hierarchical recurrence, while finding that a simpler single-state
recurrent Transformer can behave comparably. [Prediction Dynamics in Depth-Recurrent
Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21383) instead studies how token predictions
stabilize across shared-block applications; its direction and pairing analyses reduce
the mean earliest qualifying depth by 22.5% to 34.4%.

### Applications beyond language modeling

[Q-DEQ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24042) combines a discrete local solver with W8A8
quantization for implicit-layer time-series forecasting. It reports MSE changes between
-1.16% and +2.90%, alongside 1.80x to 3.82x fewer parameters and 4.3x to 12.8x lower
storage. [Neural Cellular Automata Learn General Features in their Hidden Channels](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21870)
shows that a roughly 9,800-parameter reused update rule develops orthogonal,
scale-invariant topological features that transfer across MNIST variants.

[REFINEPPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21108) repeatedly applies one residual policy
network to refine a continuous action proposal and improves 13 of 14 reported control
tasks. [PhGS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20623) applies one shared learned 3D Gaussian
refiner for four steps after a frozen feed-forward backbone, removing roughly half the
Gaussians while largely preserving reconstruction quality.

## Near misses

- [2609.26590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26590), [2609.23366](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23366),
  and [2609.23900](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23900) concern recurrence primarily across
  tokens, space, or ordinary sequence state; they do not clearly establish reused
  learned depth inside one forward process.
- [2609.22228](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22228) discusses latent reasoning but does not
  make internal layer reuse explicit enough for the strict catalog boundary.
- [2609.25131](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25131) and [2609.26052](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26052)
  use iterative flow or diffusion-style inference, which is explicitly outside the
  catalog's recurrent-layer scope.

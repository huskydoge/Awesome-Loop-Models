# Daily Loop-Model Briefing - 2026-09-03

## Outcome

One analysis paper was added. It directly studies how tied-weight recurrent depth
changes the internal organization and causal accessibility of representations.

## Added

### Looped Transformers under the Jacobian Lens (2609.01924)

The paper adapts the Jacobian lens to a virtually unrolled computation graph and
applies the same fitting, readout, and causal-intervention protocol to two looped
language models and an untied Transformer baseline. Ouro-2.6B applies its 48-layer
stack four times; Huginn-0125 repeats a four-layer core 16 times.

Why it was added: both evaluated models explicitly reuse learned Transformer weights
across depth within one forward process, and the paper's central question is how that
recurrent structure changes internal computation.

Reader takeaway: both models develop a verbalisable and causally steerable workspace,
but recurrence changes its interfaces. Ouro reconstructs workspace content in every
loop, so writes and ablations must span all remaining loops. Huginn carries content
through all 16 recurrences, while effective reads, writes, and ablations remain local
to a window of roughly two recurrences. The study also reports that Huginn verbalises
self-computed content almost perfectly but does not verbalise externally injected
content, unlike the deeply supervised Ouro model.

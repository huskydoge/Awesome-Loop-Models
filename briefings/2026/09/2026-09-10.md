# Daily Loop-Model Briefing - 2026-09-10

## Outcome

One paper was added. Looped GPT-BERT directly studies the parameter-compute trade-off
created by applying a compact Transformer stack repeatedly along depth.

## Added

### Looped GPT-BERT (2609.09691)

Looped GPT-BERT combines causal and masked next-token training with depth-wise
parameter sharing. Its recurrent backbone carries a loop state through the same
four-layer Transformer stack, using up to twelve traversals in one model forward.
The final 12.18M-parameter model reports a BabyLM 2026 Overall Average of 35.42 and
an NLP Average of 48.48.

Why it belongs: the learned four-layer stack is explicitly reused across recurrent
depth, with the output state of each traversal fed into the next traversal.

Reader takeaway: recurrent compute helps most when moving from one to three
traversals. At twelve total layer applications, the four-layer stack looped three
times measures 55 ms, 74,128 tokens/s, and 0.51 GiB peak memory, compared with
58 ms, 70,571 tokens/s, and 0.68 GiB for the twelve-layer non-looped model. More
loops do not improve every task: Entity Tracking is often weaker, and the authors
limit their claims to one seed, roughly 12M parameters, 7.48M training words, and
128-token contexts.

Resources: [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09691),
[code](https://github.com/Survivor613/BabyLM-2026), and
[model](https://huggingface.co/Survivor613/BabyLM2026-Strict-Small-looped_GPT-BERT).

## Near misses

- [Semigroup-JEPA (2609.10464)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10464) trains an
  autoregressive latent world-model rollout. Its recurrence runs across simulated
  time rather than reusing a learned block as recurrent model depth.
- [ConvMem (2609.10441)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10441) orchestrates repeated
  full-model calls over a segment tree instead of adding an internal learned loop.
- [RiLM (2609.10305)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10305) is a sequence-time recurrent
  language model without a recurrent-depth contribution.
- [Stable Answers, Unfinished Reasoning (2609.09989)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09989)
  probes answer stability along an external generation trajectory rather than
  introducing or analyzing reused internal layers.
- [Structural Process Supervision for Latent Chain-of-Thought Reasoning
  (2609.09928)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09928) supervises latent representations
  but does not establish repeated application of a learned internal operator.
- [Settling (2609.09682)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09682) performs test-time
  critic-guided equilibrium refinement; it is not a learned implicit layer, and
  learned high-dimensional validation remains open.

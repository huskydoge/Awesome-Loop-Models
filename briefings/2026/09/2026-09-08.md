# Daily Loop-Model Briefing - 2026-09-08

## Outcome

No new paper was added. Two existing catalog entries gained direct links to
first-party implementations and released checkpoints, making their architectures
easier to inspect and reproduce.

## Newly linked artifacts

### LT2: Linear-Time Looped Transformers (`2605.20670`)

The [official LT2 repository](https://github.com/chili-lab/LT2) implements the
paper's linear, sparse, and hybrid token mixers. Its looped architecture repeatedly
reuses the same Transformer-block parameters, with configurations and evaluation
entry points for the reported language-model experiments.

### Gated Recurrent Transformers (`2608.15062`)

The authors released both the
[official implementation](https://github.com/Amr-Hegazy1/gated-recurrent-transformer)
and a [checkpoint collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/Amr-Hegazy/gated-recurrent-transformer-grt).
The model retains a fixed prelude and coda while iterating one gated shared core;
the checkpoints expose the paper's isoFLOPS and isoPARAMS configurations.

## Near miss

- [DODR (`2609.04782`)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04782) repeatedly applies
  trainable matrix operators in a reasoning graph, but the proposed graph
  scheduler is not implemented and the evidence remains limited to small,
  single-seed experiments without standard benchmarks or baselines. It remains
  adjacent context rather than a catalog addition.

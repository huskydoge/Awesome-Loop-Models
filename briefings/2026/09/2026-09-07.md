# Daily Loop-Model Briefing - 2026-09-07

## Outcome

One analysis paper was added. It treats recurrent-depth reasoning models as
dynamical systems and asks why difficult problems require many more loops to settle.

## Added

### Fractal basins trap latent reasoning (2609.04963)

The study probes Equilibrium Reasoners, Fixed-Point Reasoning Models, Parcae, and
Tiny Recursive Models by perturbing their initial latent states and recording the
decoded solution after every loop. It also trains encoder-only looped Transformers
to follow the same dynamics. The authors released their
[basin-probing code](https://github.com/GilpinLab/loopscape).

Why it was added: looped and fixed-point models are the paper's central experimental
objects. Their shared learned transitions are repeatedly applied within one forward
process, and the analysis explicitly follows those latent trajectories across depth.

Reader takeaway: harder tasks produce more complex, often fractal convergence-time
basins. Slow trajectories linger near saddle points that decode to nearly correct
solutions before escaping. During training of a looped Transformer, a bifurcation
around 150,000 steps coincides with correct solving, unstable saddle directions, and
the appearance of fractal basins.

## Near miss

- 2609.04782 repeatedly applies a composite learned operator toward a fixed point,
  but its graph scheduler is not implemented, all experiments use one seed, and the
  paper lacks standard benchmarks and baselines. It remains a watchlist item rather
  than a catalog addition.

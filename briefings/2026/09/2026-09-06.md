# Daily Loop-Model Briefing - 2026-09-06

## Outcome

Six papers were added. Together they broaden shared-computation models along three
axes: adaptive or parallel language-model computation, implicit learned operators
for scientific simulation, and task-specific reuse in OCR and vision.

## Adaptive and parallel language-model compute

### RecurTrace (2609.03379)

RecurTrace turns three middle Qwen3 layers into a fully weight-tied recurrent block.
Each looped layer can attend to its own states from earlier iterations, while an
oracle-distilled halting head decides when another loop is useful. On MathQA it
reaches 56.9% at an average of two loops, 2.2 points above the best fixed depth at
matched compute; gains also grow across the reported 0.6B-8B scale range.

Why it belongs: both the latent state transition and the new loop-memory access are
inside one recurrent forward process, with the same learned block reused to variable
depth.

### Free Pause Tokens (2609.03807)

Free Pause Tokens separates persistent context state from next-token prediction. A
state stream writes the usual per-layer KV states, while a prediction stream shares
every backbone weight and queries those states without adding a sequence position or
KV entry. The full method improves 1B-model cross-entropy by about 2.8 centinats;
late-phase training retains most of the gain at 1.33x wall-clock cost, although the
full variant is slightly negative under a strict iso-FLOP comparison.

Why it belongs: the forward graph contains two coordinated streams over the same
learned layer stack, making parameter sharing a central architectural mechanism.

## Implicit scientific computation

### Time Without Timesteps (2609.03358)

This work learns full-trajectory surrogate operators for individual subsystem types
and composes them through a Newton-Krylov fixed-point solve. Coupled trajectories are
recovered in 4-10 solver iterations in examples where the reference integrator uses
1,500 time steps, while GMRES implicit differentiation keeps memory independent of
solver depth.

Why it belongs: the learned trajectory operators define the central fixed-point map
and are repeatedly evaluated during one implicit forward solve.

### Equation Recast (2609.02982)

Equation recast absorbs analytically known PDE-parameter changes into an effective
source, then repeatedly applies one learned canonical inverse until the target
solution becomes self-consistent. The method supports zero-shot parameter transfer
across several PDE families and four tokamak geometries; slow or failed convergence
also exposes regimes where the learned continuation path is unreliable.

Why it belongs: a single learned neural operator is explicitly reused within the
fixed-point inference algorithm rather than replaced by parameter-specific models.

## Application-facing shared computation

### Jina-OCR-v1 (2609.03181)

Jina-OCR-v1 adds FastMTP to an OCR vision-language model. One dense draft block is
recursively reused for three speculative prediction depths, while greedy verification
keeps the output lossless relative to ordinary greedy decoding. The system reports
91.14 on OmniDocBench, 83.4 on olmOCR-Bench, and roughly doubles decoding speed on an
NVIDIA L4.

Why it belongs: the draft computation has explicit recurrent depth and constant
parameters because the same learned block proposes each successive token.

### ProgResViT (2609.03216)

ProgResViT processes easy images with a narrow, low-resolution subnetwork and sends
uncertain examples through wider, higher-resolution rounds. Earlier tokens are
projected and fused into later rounds, while the same ViT blocks are reused with
progress-conditioned gates. Its strongest reported operating point cuts average
ImageNet compute by 31.1% while reducing top-1 accuracy by only 0.03 points.

Why it belongs: progressive refinement carries hidden representations through
repeated applications of one shared Transformer backbone inside an adaptive forward
pipeline.

## Near misses

- 2609.04131 repeatedly evaluates a frozen vision-language model while optimizing an
  external latent memory, so its loop is test-time optimization around the model.
- 2609.03445 alternates OCR diagnosis, executable edits, rendering, and reassessment;
  this is a model-and-tool workflow rather than reused internal learned depth.
- 2609.02940 combines separate ASR and language-model passes without a tied internal
  recurrent operator.
- 2609.03294 performs quasi-Newton energy optimization around a frozen world model,
  falling under the catalog's explicit energy/iterative-inference exclusion.

# Daily Loop-Model Briefing - 2026-09-13

## Outcome

Two papers were added. LoopVAE makes recurrent depth a parameter-sharing axis for
visual tokenization, and Looped Flows trains recurrent reasoners through temporally
associated denoising objectives.

## Added

### LoopVAE (2609.11516)

LoopVAE gives its encoder and decoder separate four-block cores, then reuses each
core across spatial scales and within a `[1,2,4]` recurrence schedule. This totals
28 core-block applications per branch while retaining independent resolution-changing
transitions.

Why it belongs: the same learned core is explicitly reapplied along both recurrent
depth and the model's visual hierarchy.

Reader takeaway: the 29M-parameter CNN reports 0.28 rFID and 32.54 dB PSNR with
about 65% fewer parameters than the 84M reference VAEs. The trade-off is execution
cost: 1.57x estimated MACs and 2.85x batch latency in the reported comparison.

Resource: [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11516).

### Thinking with Looped Flows (2609.11801)

Looped Flows makes a TRM-based denoiser stateful: every shared-network call predicts
a solution and updates recurrent hidden state, while an ODE or SDE step advances the
coupled probability-flow state. Training uses local denoising objectives at decreasing
noise levels rather than backpropagating through the full recurrent trajectory.

Why it belongs: probability-flow integration is part of the method, but the learned
denoiser itself carries and repeatedly updates shared recurrent state. The internal
recurrent computation is therefore central rather than an external sampling wrapper.

Reader takeaway: with the same underlying architecture, the paper reports gains over
TRM from 44.6% to 58.8% on ARC-AGI-1 and from 7.8% to 12.2% on ARC-AGI-2. On Sudoku,
accuracy rises from 74.5% at 8 inference steps to 97.9% at 128 steps, showing direct
inference-compute scaling along the learned recurrence.

Resource: [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11801).

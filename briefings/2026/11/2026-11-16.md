# Daily Loop-Model Briefing - 2026-11-16

## Outcome

One paper was added. *Folding Language Models into Themselves* is a retrofitting paper: it
builds a compact student from a pretrained model by looping shared blocks in place of groups of
adjacent layers, then distills it from the original model. It does not cite Benhao Huang's
work.

## Added

### Folding Language Models into Themselves

[arXiv:2611.18113](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18113) · Code: [myboker/concertina](https://github.com/myboker/concertina)

**TL;DR:** Concertina folds each group of N adjacent layers of a pretrained decoder into one
shared block that runs N times, and distills the result from the original model. A 2.27B fold
of Qwen3-4B trained on 22B tokens keeps 84% of the teacher on average and beats Qwen3-1.7B on
six of eight benchmarks.

- **Setup:** Qwen3-4B (36 layers) is the teacher, and the main student folds every two
  adjacent layers into one block, giving 18 blocks and 2.27B parameters. Students are distilled
  with forward KL to the teacher plus cross-entropy on a 22B-token mixture of math,
  multiple-choice, academic and web text. Evaluation covers commonsense tasks, MMLU, MMLU-Redux,
  MMLU-Pro, GPQA-diamond, BBH, GSM8K and MATH. A second run folds Qwen3-8B in groups of three.
- **Mechanism / Method:** The shared weight of each block starts as a copy of the group's
  first layer. Every later loop step adds a low-rank correction initialized from the SVD of the
  difference between its original layer and the shared weight, so the folded model starts close
  to the teacher. A per-token gate mixes the step corrections, and RMSNorm scales stay separate
  per step. All weights are trained.
- **Results:** The 2.27B model keeps 92% of the teacher's commonsense score, 90% of GSM8K and
  88% of MATH, and scores higher than Qwen3-1.7B on six of eight benchmarks. Against a truncated
  dense model of 2.31B parameters distilled on the same 20B tokens, it scores 8 to 11 points
  higher on the MMLU family and 0.718 against 0.017 on GSM8K. With the authors' own recipe on
  197M tokens it reaches 0.498 on the commonsense average, above Relaxed Recursive Transformers
  (0.480) and FoldGPT (0.430) trained with their published recipes on 2B tokens. Under a shared
  recipe, tying adjacent layers beats Relaxed Recursive Transformers' cycle tying (0.535 against
  0.521 at 2B tokens). Folding Qwen3-8B into 3.68B parameters keeps 91% of its commonsense score
  and 83% of GSM8K.
- **Caveat:** The comparison with prior folding methods mixes structure with training recipe,
  because Concertina distills from the teacher while the baselines use cross-entropy. The
  controlled ablations run only up to 2B tokens and score the commonsense tasks on at most 1,000
  examples each. Only Qwen3 teachers are folded, and the loop count is fixed by the folding
  ratio.
- **Why added:** Within one forward pass, each shared block is executed N times in place of N
  original layers, with per-step low-rank adapters. The paper's contribution is how to retrofit
  a pretrained model into this looped form.

## Near misses

- **RecMem** ([2611.17921](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17921)): compresses history segments
  into memory slots with forget-gated updates and shares lower layers between its encoder and
  decoder paths; this is segment-level memory recurrence, and no block is run repeatedly on its
  own output within a forward pass.
- **RecurIR** ([2611.18006](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18006)): "recursive" refers to an
  image-restoration agent that revises its tool-use policy over rounds, an agent loop rather
  than a reused internal block.
